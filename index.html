Pipeline

From receipt to rescue, automated.

A continuous operational loop closing the gap between dock intake and zero edible waste.

  1. 01

    Dock Intake

    Connect POS or drop a batch CSV. Received dates and expiry timestamps ingest automatically.

  2. 02

    FEFO Rotation

    Inventory ranks by days to expiry. Staff pick lists keep front displays fresh first.

  3. 03

    Spoilage Scoring

    Machine learning evaluates sales velocity and weather to forecast shelf-life risks daily.

  4. 04

    Surplus Rescue

    Dynamic markdowns, flash retail bundles, and food rescue donations trigger before waste occurs.

  5. 05

    Audit & ESG

    Recovery values and diverted kilograms log instantly to your sustainability ledger.
