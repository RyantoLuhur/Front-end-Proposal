Four systems. One problem solved.

Automated inventory intelligence from dock to checkout. Stop food waste before it leaves the shelf.

01
FEFO

Expiry-first inventory

Shelved by days to expiry, not arrival date. Oldest batches stay front-facing so nothing is forgotten at the back.

02
ML Risk Model

Spoilage risk prediction

Every batch scored 0 to 1 against velocity, weather, and shelf life to trigger markdowns days before spoilage.

03
7-Day Projection

Demand forecasting

Sales projections matched against current stock so buyers can halt replenishment before surplus builds.

04
Telemetry

Operations dashboard

Track clearance speed, food salvaged, and value recovered with an automated audit trail for ESG reporting.
